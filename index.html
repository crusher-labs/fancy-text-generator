Cool fonts you can paste anywhere

These are not actual fonts: each style rewrites your letters as different Unicode characters (mathematical bold, script, fraktur, circled letters and so on). Because they are real characters, they survive copy and paste into places that never let you pick a font: Instagram bios and captions, TikTok, X and Facebook names, Discord usernames and statuses, WhatsApp, YouTube comments, even plain text files.

Type in the box and every style updates live. One click on Copy puts that version on your clipboard.

A note on compatibility

Most modern phones and browsers render all of these. A few styles (squared, negative circled) depend on the reader's device fonts, so double-check anything important. Screen readers may spell some styles letter by letter, so use them for decoration, not for whole paragraphs. More free tools live on the Web Utility Tools hub.

Why does the text still paste as fancy letters?

Because they are Unicode characters, not styling. The look travels with the text itself, which is exactly why it works in bios and usernames where formatting is stripped.

Will these work in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Copy a style, open your profile, edit bio, paste. Instagram accepts Unicode characters in bios, captions, comments and (mostly) usernames.

Numbers or symbols did not change. Why?

Some Unicode alphabets only exist for A-Z letters. Anything without a fancy equivalent (most punctuation, emoji, non-Latin scripts) passes through unchanged.
